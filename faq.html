FAQ

Frequently asked questions related to the Knowledge Management portal

General Questions

How do I upload a SOP or policy document?
Go to the “Submit Document” page, complete the metadata fields, and upload the document for approval.
Who can access restricted documents?
Access depends on your role. Sensitive documents are available only to authorised staff.
How do I find an expert in a specific area?
Use the Expert Directory to search by skill, department, or expertise area.

Learning & Training

Where can I access training materials?
Training modules and onboarding guides are available under the Training & Onboarding section.
Is completion of training mandatory?
Yes, mandatory training modules must be completed to comply with internal policies.

Support & Help

What should I do if I face a system issue?
Visit the Contact Support page to submit a support ticket or chat with the support team.
Can I suggest improvements to the KM portal?
Yes, you can submit your suggestions through the Feedback page.
