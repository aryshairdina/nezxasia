Suspicious Transaction Reporting SOP

Internal

Executive Summary

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the mandatory steps for identifying, documenting, and reporting suspicious transactions in accordance with the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Act 2001. All frontline staff in Malaysia branches must adhere to this workflow.
